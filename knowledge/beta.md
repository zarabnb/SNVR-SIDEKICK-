# SNVR BETA TEST PROGRAMME

Source: SNVR announcement dated November 6, 2026.
Application page: https://snvr.org/apply

## Overview

SNVR announced a limited Beta Test for 100 selected participants.

The programme is intended to let selected users access available
SNVR features, test the experience, report problems and provide feedback.

SNVR is described in the announcement as being built on Oasis Sapphire.

## Important Dates

Applications open: November 6, 2026.
Applications close: November 14, 2026.
Available places: 100 selected users.
Testing period: November 16–30, 2026.

These dates are specific to the announced Beta programme.
Do not assume applications remain open after November 14.

## Application Requirements

The announcement requests:

1. Email address.
2. Telegram username.
3. X username.
4. Country of residence.

Official application:
https://snvr.org/apply

## What Participants Will Test

### SNVR Messenger
Test messaging and payment functionality within Telegram.

### Transactions and Payments
Test sending and receiving funds.
Check confirmations, delivery, delays and errors.

### Relay Engine
Test multi-path transaction routing.
Observe transaction submission, completion and errors.

### Feedback and Bug Reports
Participants are encouraged to report:
- What they were trying to do.
- What happened.
- Any error messages.
- Suggested improvements.
- Positive experiences.

## Beta Rewards

The announcement describes a points-based incentive system.

Points are intended to be converted into SNVR tokens.

A separate campaign may provide additional points and stablecoin rewards
to selected high-performing participants.

Detailed conversion rules and reward conditions are to be communicated
separately.

Do not invent token conversion rates or reward guarantees.

## ROSE Gas Support

Every selected Beta tester is announced to receive 1 ROSE
for testing purposes.

Estimated gas costs:
- Standard transaction: approximately 0.02 ROSE.
- Routing transaction: approximately 0.05 ROSE.

Actual costs may vary with network conditions.

The announcement suggests approximately $8 worth of additional ROSE
for users intending to continue beyond the Beta.

## Participation Expectations

Participants are expected to:
- Use available features.
- Test transactions where possible.
- Report bugs.
- Provide honest feedback.
- Help improve the product.
