# GETTING TO KNOW SNVR

## What is SNVR?

SNVR stands for Stealth Network for Vault Relay.

It is a privacy-focused financial ecosystem built on Oasis Sapphire, within the ROSE ecosystem.

The idea behind SNVR is simple: your financial activities should not automatically be exposed for everyone to see.

On many traditional blockchains, wallet balances and transactions are publicly visible. SNVR uses confidential blockchain technology to give users greater control over their financial information.

It brings messaging, payments, asset management, swapping and private transaction routing into one connected ecosystem.

Its guiding philosophy is:

"Your Money, Your Business."

## What is Oasis Sapphire?

Oasis Sapphire is a privacy-focused blockchain environment that supports confidential smart contracts.

Unlike ordinary transparent smart contracts, Sapphire allows developers to build applications where certain data and contract operations can remain confidential.

SNVR uses this environment as part of its approach to private balances, transfers and financial activity.

In simple terms, Oasis Sapphire provides the underlying technology that helps SNVR build financial applications with greater confidentiality. It does not mean every activity is automatically invisible or that absolute anonymity is guaranteed.

## Who is SNVR built for?

SNVR is designed for people who want greater control over their digital assets and financial information.

This includes people who:
- Want to reduce the public exposure of their wallet activity.
- Need to send, receive and manage digital assets.
- Want messaging and financial services connected in one ecosystem.
- Prefer greater control over when and how their financial information is disclosed.
- Want to use swaps and transaction routing with privacy-focused features.

SNVR's broader goal is to make financial privacy more practical and accessible in everyday digital asset use.

## How does the SNVR ecosystem work?

SNVR is built around four main pillars:

**S — Stealth-style Addressing**

Designed to reduce the public visibility of wallet balances and transaction relationships through confidential blockchain state and fresh payment patterns.

**N — Network and Relay Nodes**

Provides the communication and routing infrastructure connecting SNVR services through distributed nodes and encrypted connections.

**V — Vault**

SNVR's asset management and confidentiality concept. It is designed to give users greater control over their assets and who can access related financial information. Its capabilities are intended to develop as the ecosystem matures.

**R — Relay Engine**

Routes transactions through multiple paths to reduce straightforward connections between senders and destinations. It is designed to make ordinary transaction tracking more difficult.

## Main SNVR Products

### SNVR Messenger

A Telegram-oriented experience combining messaging and payment functionality.

The goal is to let users communicate and interact with financial services in one connected experience instead of constantly switching between applications.

### GhostSwap

GhostSwap is SNVR's automated market maker (AMM) for swapping supported assets on Oasis Sapphire.

Its documented swap fee is 0.3%:
- 0.2% goes to liquidity providers.
- 0.1% goes to SNVR protocol operations.

### SNVR Vault

The Vault focuses on asset management, confidentiality and controlled visibility.

Its design includes multi-signature-style controls and confidential asset information.

### Relay Engine

The Relay Engine provides multi-path transaction routing.

Its documented fee is 1% on SNVR routed through the dedicated Relay contract:
- 0.5% is burned.
- 0.5% funds protocol operations.

The burn mechanism connects token supply reduction to actual routed transaction activity.

## How does SNVR protect financial privacy?

SNVR uses Oasis Sapphire's confidential smart-contract environment to support private balances and transfers.

It also uses encrypted communication infrastructure for connections between clients, relays and APIs.

An important part of its approach is selective signed access.

Sometimes users need to demonstrate their transaction history or prove their funds to an exchange or financial partner.

SNVR allows users to authorise specific disclosure by signing with MetaMask. This process does not give the receiving party permission to move their funds.

The goal is to combine everyday financial privacy with the ability to provide proof when needed.

## SNVR Tokenomics

SNVR has a stated total supply of 1 billion tokens.

The allocation is:

- Presale: 20% — 200 million.
- Liquidity: 25% — 250 million.
- Ecosystem and Community: 20% — 200 million.
- Team and Advisors: 15% — 150 million.
- Early Investors: 10% — 100 million.
- Strategic Reserve: 10% — 100 million.

SNVR is designed to support activities across its ecosystem, including swaps, liquidity, payments, transaction routing and community incentives.

The Relay Engine's burn mechanism is intended to reduce supply over time. Actual burns depend on routed transaction volume.

## Where is SNVR heading?

SNVR's roadmap describes several development stages:

- Closed beta and product development, including Messenger, the Mini App, sending, swapping and privacy routing.
- Presale, targeted for December 2026.
- Exchange listing and liquidity expansion.
- Future payment infrastructure, including the SNVR Shield Card, automated top-ups and mobile payment integrations targeted for 2027.

These are roadmap targets, not confirmation that every feature is already available.

## The Bigger Picture

SNVR aims to bring private financial activity into everyday digital life.

Instead of treating messaging, payments, swapping, asset management and transaction privacy as separate experiences, it connects them into one ecosystem.

Its long-term vision is to give people more control over their money, their financial information and how they interact with digital financial services.

The central idea is that financial privacy should be practical, accessible and controlled by the user.
